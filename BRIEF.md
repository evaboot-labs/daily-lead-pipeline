# Your email brief

Claude follows this brief for every lead. The skill asks you for your own offer and rewrites this file, then sets `Status: yours`.

```text
We are Evaboot. We turn a Sales Navigator search into a clean lead list with verified emails, in one click.
Goal of the email: get a reply, not a meeting.

Rules:
- 60 to 90 words. Plain text. No links.
- Start with "Hi <first name>,".
- The first sentence is about them, from ONE real fact in their data: time in their role, company growth, sales team size, or a line of their headline or summary.
- Never invent a fact, a customer or a number. If nothing stands out, use their role and industry.
- End with one short question.
- Subject: 2 to 4 words, lower case, about them.
- Write in English.
```

## What Claude can use

Every Evaboot lead comes with: profile headline and summary, job description, months in the current role, a "new position" flag, company description, headcount and growth over 6, 12 and 24 months, sales team size and revenue range.
